OWNER DECISION

New files are being processed, but unchanged historical HELD items
cause later invocations to report PARTIAL, and the AutoSys job is
reported as failed.

An otherwise healthy invocation must not fail solely because it
encounters unresolved HELD items carried over from previous runs.

This changes the invocation-completion contract only. It does not
resolve those items or make their deliveries complete.

This instruction supersedes only the earlier requirement that
unchanged historical HOLD automatically makes every later invocation
non-successful. Preserve the remaining unknown-outcome protections.

2. Implement the smallest justified correction

Keep these meanings distinct:
- Outcome of this invocation's execution.
- Outstanding business work across deliveries.

Required behavior:
- Historical HELD items only, with successful discovery and no current
  execution error: successful polling/no-new-work outcome using the
  existing success exit convention; historical backlog remains visible.
- Historical HELD items plus successfully completed eligible work:
  successful invocation; historical items remain unresolved.
- A newly ambiguous submission during this invocation: preserve the
  existing non-success handling and no-resubmission protection.
- A current technical, configuration, storage, publication or archive
  failure: preserve the appropriate non-success result.
- Confirmed terminal provider-content failures: preserve the existing
  INVALIDS and durable completion contract.

Do not implement a blanket PARTIAL-to-success conversion.

Distinguish carried-over HELD items using reliable run/work identity
and state observed at the invocation boundary. Do not rely only on
timestamps, unchanged total counts, filename matching, or whether the
delivery itself is old.

Re-reading or rendering an old HELD item must not make it a newly
failed item. Conversely, current failures on resumed old work must
not be hidden because that work predates the invocation.

Limit the exception to historical, non-executable unknown-outcome
holds. Do not silently exempt PENDING_TECHNICAL, unfinished executable
work, missing artifacts or failed delivery obligations.

Failure to read the ledger or discover work must never become
"no new work" success.

Preserve archive/publication eligibility and existing delivery gates.
